Privacy Policy for Sky Cluck

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Sky Cluck ("the app") is a casual arcade game published by Rafat Bahar. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Nickname (optional). If you choose to submit your score to the global leaderboard, you will be asked to enter a nickname. This nickname, along with your score, is stored and displayed publicly on the leaderboard. You are not required to submit a score, and you should not use your real name.

Local high scores. Your personal high scores are stored only on your device and are not transmitted anywhere.

Advertising data. The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, approximate location, and other data as described in Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy, in order to show and measure ads. We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob - ad serving. Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Supabase - stores the global leaderboard, nickname and score only

Children's Privacy

Sky Cluck is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Deletion

To request removal of a leaderboard entry, contact us using the information below.
