Privacy Policy for Sky Cluck

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Sky Cluck ("the app") is a casual arcade game published by Rafat Bahar. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Nickname and leaderboard entry (optional). If you submit a Classic Cluck score to the global leaderboard, you will be asked to enter a nickname. Your nickname and best score are stored and displayed publicly on the leaderboard. You are not required to submit a score, and you should not use your real name.

Player ID. The first time the app needs one, it creates a random player ID on your device and stores it with your leaderboard entry. It only lets you change your nickname without losing your score. It is not linked to your name, email, Google account or any other identifier, and it is never shown to other players.

Game progress on your device. Your personal high scores, feather coins, chickens, daily missions, starters and settings are stored only on your device and are not sent to us.

Advertising data. The app shows ads using Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for an in-game reward. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, approximate location, and other data as described in Google's Privacy Policy, in order to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings (Ad Privacy Choices). We do not receive or store this data ourselves.

Google Play Games (Android). Classic Cluck scores can also appear on a Google Play Games leaderboard. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, Google receives your score and shows it with your Play Games profile (such as your gamer name and avatar). This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your Google account details.

Purchases. If you buy Remove Ads, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases are active on your device; RevenueCat receives purchase information and an anonymous app user ID. We do not receive your payment details.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob - ads and ad consent. Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Games Services - Google Play Games leaderboard (Android). Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing - the Remove Ads purchase. Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat - checks purchase status. Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Supabase - stores the global leaderboard: nickname, best score and random player ID only. Policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

Sky Cluck is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Deletion

To remove your leaderboard entry, contact us using the information below and tell us your nickname. Game progress stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. To remove your Google Play Games data, use your Google account settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
